## FrameOps — Window & Door Operations Management

**Project / Program Delivery · Digital Transformation · Value Stream Design · Cross-functional Workflow Automation**

End-to-end interactive operations command center demonstrating a customer storefront, admin and department-specific views, guided chat-style help, scheduled appointments, supplier ETAs and delay notifications, invoice and service-case tracking, and customer satisfaction through optional Google review invitations after warranty closeout.

**What it showcases:** Lean flow, visible handoffs and bottlenecks, delivery dependencies, role-based work queues, in-app notifications, homeowner transparency, and measurable business outcomes. Incorporates practical value-stream and Lean Portfolio Management principles, aligned with enterprise Agile and SAFe delivery thinking.
